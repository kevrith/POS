FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does KASTRA work without internet?
Yes. KASTRA is offline-first — all sales, inventory, and payments are stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB. Data syncs automatically to the cloud when your connection is restored. You can sell through power cuts, weak signal, and full outages.
Is KASTRA officially approved by KRA for eTIMS?
KASTRA integrates with KRA's official eTIMS API (Tax Invoice Management System). Every sale triggers an automatic invoice submission. We maintain a pending queue for offline periods — invoices sync when internet is restored.
How does M-Pesa integration work?
KASTRA uses Safaricom's official Daraja API. When a customer pays by M-Pesa, you enter the amount and their phone number — they receive an STK push on their phone immediately. Alternatively, they can scan a dynamic QR code. Both methods confirm payment in real time at the POS.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes. You can upgrade at any time from Settings → Subscription. Downgrading is also possible at the end of your billing period. All your data is retained regardless of plan changes.
Is there a setup fee or contract?
No setup fees, no long-term contracts. KASTRA is billed monthly and you can cancel anytime. Your 30-day free trial gives you access to all features on your chosen plan.
What hardware do I need?
KASTRA runs in any modern web browser — no special hardware required. It works on tablets, laptops, and desktop PCs. For receipts, it supports standard 58mm/80mm thermal receipt printers via USB. You can also use a USB barcode scanner or your device camera for scanning.
How is my data protected?
All sensitive credentials (M-Pesa API keys, eTIMS certificates) are encrypted with AES-256-CBC before storage. KASTRA is compliant with Kenya's Data Protection Act (KDPA 2019) — customers can request data deletion which triggers a full anonymisation process with audit trail. Access is protected by JWT tokens, PIN auto-lock, and optional TOTP two-factor authentication.
