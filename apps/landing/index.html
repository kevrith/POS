FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does KASTRA work without internet? +
Yes — KASTRA is offline-first. All sales, inventory, and payments are stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB (via Dexie.js). Data syncs automatically to the cloud when your connection is restored. You can sell through power cuts, weak signal, and full outages without losing a single transaction. The desktop (Tauri) app also includes a local SQLite database for fully disconnected operation.
Is KASTRA KRA eTIMS compliant? +
KASTRA integrates with KRA's official eTIMS v2.0 API (Tax Invoice Management System). Every checkout triggers an automatic invoice submission in the background. If the submission fails (e.g. no internet), the receipt is watermarked "PENDING KRA SUBMISSION" and the invoice is queued for retry. Once connectivity is restored, the queue processes automatically. KASTRA also supports eTIMS void and amendment (orgInvcNo) per KRA specification.
How does M-Pesa integration work? +
KASTRA uses Safaricom's official Daraja API. When a customer pays by M-Pesa, you enter the amount — they receive an STK push on their phone immediately. Alternatively, a dynamic QR code (changing per transaction) is generated for self-service scanning. Both methods provide real-time confirmation at the POS. Bank transfer, Paystack card, gift cards, and split payments are also supported.
What hardware do I need? +
KASTRA runs in any modern browser — no proprietary hardware required. Works on Windows/Mac/Linux laptops and tablets. For receipts, standard 58mm/80mm ESC/POS thermal printers via USB or network are supported. USB and camera barcode scanners work out of the box. The Tauri desktop app provides a native Windows/Linux experience with hardware receipt printing. A pole display (customer-facing screen) is also supported via any second monitor.
What is the difference between Monthly, Annual, and Lifetime pricing? +
Monthly billing is flexible — cancel or change plans anytime. Annual billing is paid upfront for 12 months at the price of 10 (2 months free). Lifetime (Pay Once) is a one-time purchase that gives you the software forever, includes 1 year of all updates (including KRA compliance patches), and then an optional annual maintenance fee applies from year 2. Lifetime plans include the full desktop (Tauri) app which can run completely offline.
How is my business data protected? +
All sensitive credentials — M-Pesa API keys, eTIMS certificates, Africa's Talking keys — are encrypted with AES-256-CBC before storage. KASTRA is compliant with Kenya's Data Protection Act (KDPA 2019): customers can request data deletion, which triggers full PII anonymisation with a signed audit trail. Access is protected by JWT tokens (15-min expiry), PIN auto-lock on inactivity, optional TOTP two-factor authentication, and Google OAuth. All audit actions are recorded in a tamper-evident chain.
Can I switch plans or cancel anytime? +
Yes. Upgrade at any time from Settings → Subscription. Downgrading takes effect at the end of your current billing period. Cancelling monthly stops the next charge. Annual plans are non-refundable after the first 7 days. All your data is retained regardless of plan changes — we never delete your data when you downgrade.
